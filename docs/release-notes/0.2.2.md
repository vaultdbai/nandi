## Nandi 0.2.2

Download from https://vaultdb.ai (or below). Requires Docker.

Nandi 0.2.2 opens VaultDB.ai's public relay: work with people outside your company, or a team with no relay of its own, and keep every request sealed end to end. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/vaultdbai/nandi/blob/v0.2.2/CHANGELOG.md).

### Headline changes

- **Join the public relay.** In setup or in Network › Connection, click **Join the public relay**. You join with the Outlook or Gmail you connected: no sign-in, no join code. Make a group, invite people by email, and your Nandis can ask each other for help.
- **Offered in setup.** The network step starts on the public relay. Nothing joins until you click **Join the public relay**.
- **Free, with fair limits.** Up to 10 groups, 50 people per group and 500 messages a day, across all your Nandis.
- **Private by design.** Requests and answers are sealed between the two Nandis. The relay keeps your email, your Nandi's public key, your groups and who asked whom, never what was said. See https://vaultdb.ai/privacy.html.

### Upgrading from 0.2.1

Accept the update, or install 0.2.2 over 0.2.1. Nothing else changes: the public relay is offered, never joined for you, and Nandi works as before without it.

### Known issues

- **No free trial yet.** Use your own Claude sign-in or a model key.
- **Google may say "Google hasn't verified this app"** when you connect Gmail. Nandi is waiting for Google's review. Click **Advanced**, then **Go to Nandi**. Nandi reads mail read-only, on your computer.
- **Gemini CLI** can't run in the container yet, and **Codex** can't use a ChatGPT login or Azure OpenAI.

### Installation

- **macOS**: download the `.dmg` and drag Nandi to Applications.
- **Windows**: `-setup.exe` (no admin rights needed) or `.msi` for IT deployment (`msiexec /i VaultDB-Nandi-windows-x64.msi /qn`).
- **Linux**: download the `.AppImage`, make it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it.

Existing users get an update notification once this release is published.
