## Nandi 0.2.3

Download from https://vaultdb.ai (or below). Requires Docker.

Nandi 0.2.3 works on day one, even without an AI account: every computer gets 100,000 free tokens. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/vaultdbai/nandi/blob/v0.2.3/CHANGELOG.md).

### Headline changes

- **Free tokens to start.** With no model of your own, Nandi uses 100,000 free tokens on DeepSeek Flash. There's no sign-up. Connect Outlook or Gmail and all your computers share one allowance.
- **Your own model always comes first.** A Claude sign-in or a key of yours is used before the free tokens, and they switch off once your own model works. Turn them back on in Settings › Models › Free tokens while some are left.
- **No key in the app.** The free tokens run through VaultDB.ai's server, which holds the key and counts the tokens. Nandi signs each call with this computer's own key.

### Upgrading from 0.2.2

Accept the update, or install 0.2.3 over 0.2.2. If you already use your own model, nothing changes.

### Known issues

- **The free tokens may pause for the day** when everyone together has used the day's share. They come back at midnight UTC.
- **Google may say "Google hasn't verified this app"** when you connect Gmail. Click **Advanced**, then **Go to Nandi**. Nandi reads mail read-only, on your computer.
- **Gemini CLI** can't run in the container yet, and **Codex** can't use a ChatGPT login or Azure OpenAI.

### Installation

- **macOS**: download the `.dmg` and drag Nandi to Applications.
- **Windows**: `-setup.exe` (no admin rights needed) or `.msi` for IT deployment (`msiexec /i VaultDB-Nandi-windows-x64.msi /qn`).
- **Linux**: download the `.AppImage`, make it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it.

Existing users get an update notification once this release is published.
