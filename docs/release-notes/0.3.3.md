## Nandi 0.3.3

Download from https://vaultdb.ai (or below). Requires Docker.

Nandi 0.3.3 shows where each to-do came from, and lets you finish or hand off a to-do from your phone. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/vaultdbai/nandi/blob/v0.3.3/CHANGELOG.md).

### New

- **To-dos show where they came from.** A to-do Nandi adds from an email or event reads "From an email · Lena · Deposit". **Details** shows when it came, to which mailbox, the lines that ask and why it matters.
- **Done and Hand to Nandi on your phone.** Each to-do on your paired phone has both buttons, and both ask for your PIN. Hand to Nandi starts a phone chat you can follow, and your phone's controls still apply.

### Known limits

- Details show on new to-dos only.
- The installer isn't code-signed yet. Windows says "unknown publisher": choose More info, then Run anyway.
