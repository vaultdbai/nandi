## Nandi 0.3.2

Download from https://vaultdb.ai (or below). Requires Docker.

Nandi 0.3.2 lets you ask Nandi from your phone, with controls for what each phone may do, makes Stop stop everything, and brings your colleagues into the Nandi box with `@`. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/vaultdbai/nandi/blob/v0.3.2/CHANGELOG.md).

### New

- **Ask Nandi from your phone.** What you ask on your paired phone becomes a Nandi chat on your computer, sealed end to end, marked "From your phone" in Live and Recent. Needs phone app version 8 at vaultdb.ai/pair.
- **Phone controls.** Settings › Your devices sets what each phone may do:
  - **Do, with your yes** (the default): every action waits for your approval.
  - **Answer only:** it reads and answers, with no actions.
  - **Off:** the phone only approves.
  - At any setting, a phone never makes agents or automations, gives new access, changes settings or unlocks a Lock box key that asks first.
- **Stop means stop.** Stop, or typing just "stop", ends the run and everything it started. If its browser is open, Nandi asks whether to close it, and closes it after 2 minutes with no answer.
- **@people and @groups.** With your company's relay, `@` lists colleagues and groups too. `@Sam …` sends Sam a request; a card shows it sent, seen and answered.
- **Examples that teach.** The examples above the box change each time, in your language, with what you haven't tried first.

### Changed

- **The left menu:** Nandi, Inbox, My notes, New chat and your chats at the top; Library, Network and Settings at the bottom.

### Known limits

- The installer isn't code-signed yet. Windows says "unknown publisher": choose More info, then Run anyway.
